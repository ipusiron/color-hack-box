What is the color wheel?

What is the color wheel?A circular arrangement of hues is. Colors are not independent,continuously connected I have

It starts with red, moves through orange, yellow, green, blue, purple, and finally returns to red again. The color wheel is a visual representation of this color continuity.

0° red 30° 60° yellow 90° 120° green 150° 180° cyan 210° 240° blue 270° 300° purple 330°

Understanding the color wheel makes it easier to find color relationships and harmonious color schemes.

What is complementary color?

complementary color In the color wheel,colors that are opposite each other It means.

Hover your mouse over the color wheel to display complementary colors

Complementary color combinations have the following characteristics:

  • strong contrast: Complement each other and have a great visual impact
  • Color scheme that stands out:Effective for areas where you want to attract attention in advertisements and web design
  • Be careful not to overuse: If you use it a lot, it will become visually tiring.

Examples of typical complementary colors: red and green, blue and orange, yellow and purple

Relationship between RGB and HSL

RGB and HSL (three elements of color) are models that express the same color in different ways. Both cover the same color space, but differ in their ease of intuitive operation for humans.

point of view RGB HSL
Expression method Intensity of the three primary colors of light Hue/saturation/lightness
intuitiveness mechanical/technical Close to human color recognition
brightness adjustment Change all 3 values Adjustable with L only
color change Calculate the ratio of three values Just change H
Purpose Hardware, image processing design, CSS
