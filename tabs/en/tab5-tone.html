💡 Benefits of understanding tone

  • Color scheme with a sense of unity: It is easier to harmonize by using the same tone
  • Impression control: Adjust the atmosphere by changing the tone
  • Effective combination: Create contrast by combining different tones
  • Optimization by target: Tone selection according to age group and purpose
🌸 Pale light (bright and soft tone)

Pale

High brightness (80-95%) x low saturation (10-30%)

pale red
pale orange
pale yellow
pale green
pale blue
pale purple

Characteristics and impression

Light and gentle color. It is especially bright among pastel colors and gives a soft impression.

  • impression: gentle, soft, gentle, neat, feminine
  • Usage example: Baby products, women's products, spring design, background color
  • Points to note: Since it is close to white, it is difficult to see against a white background.

Light

High brightness (75-90%) × Medium saturation (40-60%)

light red
light orange
light yellow
light green
light blue
light purple

Characteristics and impression

Bright and refreshing colors. The color is clearer and more vibrant than pale.

  • impression: Refreshing, bright, light, clean, friendly
  • Usage example: Children's content, casual brands, spring/summer design
  • Points to note: If used extensively, it may give the impression of being too light.

🍂 Dal Greish (calm tone)

Dull

Medium brightness (40-60%) x low-medium saturation (30-50%)

Characteristics and impression

Dull neutral color. It's neither too flashy nor too plain, it has a calm and mature atmosphere.

  • impression: Calm, sober, mature, natural, chic
  • Usage example: Business sites, brands for adults, fall/winter design
  • Points to note: Add accents so it doesn't look too plain

Characteristics and impression

Color close to gray. It is almost achromatic, giving it a very subdued and sophisticated impression.

  • impression: Understated, sophisticated, modern, classy, ​​minimalist
  • Usage example: Background color, luxury branding, modern design, UI elements
  • Points to note: It may be difficult to distinguish from achromatic colors

💡 Key points of tone color scheme

  • I want a sense of unity: Align in the same tone
  • I want sharpness: combine different tones
  • By target: For children → Bright, for adults → Dull/Grayish
  • Seasonality: Spring/summer → pale/light, autumn/winter → dark/deep
  • By industry: Medical → Pale, Finance → Dark, Entertainment → Vivid
