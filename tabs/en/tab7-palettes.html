How to use color palettes

1. Choose a palette

Choose the optimal palette based on the purpose, industry, season, and atmosphere. It is OK to combine multiple palettes.

2. Decide on the ratio

the color you chose70:25:5 or60:30:10 Arrange in the ratio of

  • Base color: Choose the brightest color or white
  • Main color: Choose the main color of your palette
  • Accent color: choose the most vivid color

3. Try placing it

Apply your chosen color scheme to your design and check for balance.

Header (main color)
Content area (base color)
Footer (sub color)

4. Adjust

Make fine adjustments while actually observing. Check out the points below:

  • Is the text easy to read? (Is the contrast ratio sufficient?)
  • Is it balanced? (Is any color too strong?)
  • Does it fit the purpose and atmosphere?
  • Does it resonate with the target user?

💡 Color scheme tips

  • When in doubt, blue: Versatile color that can be used in a wide range of industries
  • Start with 3 colors: Start with 3 colors, then increase as you get used to it.
  • With reference to examples: Analyze the color scheme of your favorite sites
  • Take advantage of the tools: Color matching tools such as Adobe Color and Coolors are also useful.
  • A/B test: Try multiple patterns to find the best solution
