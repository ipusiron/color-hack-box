Characteristics of achromatic colors

  • No hue: Does not belong to any position on the color wheel
  • Saturation is 0%: Absolutely no brightness
  • Changes only in brightness: From black (0%) to white (100%)
  • RGB values ​​are equal: R, G, and B have the same value, such as rgb(128, 128, 128)
  • Goes well with any color: Bring out chromatic colors

Contrast ratio and accessibility

In web design,WCAG Accessibility Guidelines It is important to maintain a contrast ratio based on

WCAG 2.2 contrast standards

level normal text large text
AA (minimum standard) 4.5:1 or higher 3:1 or more
AAA (more stringent standards) 7:1 or higher 4.5:1 or higher

For large text, we recommend normal text of 18pt or more, or bold characters of 14pt or more. AA/AAA is a standard for determining font color, and does not indicate the conformance level of the entire page.

What this test reveals: The relative luminance ratio of the two specified colors and whether the selected numerical criteria are met. The ratio is displayed to the third decimal place, but the value before rounding is used for judgment.

What this test alone doesn't tell you: The actual size and thickness of characters, the minimum ratio of images and gradations, various states such as hover, focus, and disabled, the colors that UI parts touch, and identification methods other than colors. The results do not prove WCAG conformance of the entire page.

Non-text 3:1 is a guideline for comparing the visual information of UI parts necessary for operation and the shapes necessary for understanding the content with their adjacent colors. There are exceptions for things like decorations and inactive parts.

💡 Tips for remembering color codes

🎯 Memorize sharp numbers
  • #000, #333, #666, #999, #CCC, #FFF
  • 3 digit notation is expanded 2 digits at a time (#333 → #333333)
📊 Hexadecimal correspondence

Hexadecimal numbers 0-F correspond to decimal numbers 0-255

  • #000000 = rgb(0, 0, 0)
  • #808080 = rgb(128, 128, 128)
  • #FFFFFF = rgb(255, 255, 255)
⚡ Convenient uses
  • text:#000, #333 (dark)
  • Supplementary text:#666, #999 (intermediate)
  • Background/Border:#CCC, #EEE, #F5F5F5 (bright)
