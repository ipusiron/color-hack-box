Three roles of color scheme

base color

70%

The color that occupies the largest area, such as the background or margins. It becomes the basic tone of the design and gives a calm impression.

example: white, beige, light gray

main color

25%

Colors that express your brand or theme. It sets the overall atmosphere and leaves a lasting impression.

example: Corporate corporate color, theme color

accent color

5%

The color to use for the part you want to emphasize. Attract attention and encourage action.

example: CTA button (*), important headings

*CTA (Call To Action) button: A button that prompts users to take action, such as "Buy now" or "Register for free"

70:25:5 rule

70:25:5 rule is the most basic and effective law of color ratio. It can be applied to a wide range of applications, from interior design to web design.

💡 Tips for using 70:25:5

  • Keep your base color subtle: Choose discreet colors such as white or gray
  • Express your personality with the main color: Choose colors that match your brand and purpose
  • Accent colors are complementary colors: It is effective to use complementary colors to the main color.
  • It doesn't have to be exact: You can freely make fine adjustments such as 65:30:5 or 75:20:5.

Tips for adjusting color ratio

1. Change the ratio depending on the purpose

📱 Smartphone app

Recommended ratio: 60:30:10

Improve operability with accent colors

🏢 Company website

Recommended ratio: 70:25:5

Create a sense of trust with a calm impression

🎨 Portfolio

Recommended ratio: 50:30:20

Balance that brings out the work

🛍️ EC site

Recommended ratio: 60:30:10

Accent that stimulates purchase desire

2. Prioritize visibility

  • Text readability: Ensure contrast ratio between background color and text color (AA standard: 4.5:1 or higher)
  • CTA button: Use accent colors to stand out and encourage clicks
  • Important information: Highlight with main color or accent color

3. Consider color weight

Dark colors and colors with high saturation feel "heavy", while bright colors and colors with low saturation feel "light".

💡 Final advice

  • There is no need to strictly follow: The ratio is just a guideline. Flexible adjustment according to design
  • Align the tone: If you use the same tone, it will harmonize even if the ratio is slightly different.
  • With reference to examples: Analyze the color ratio of your favorite sites
  • Leverage A/B testing: Try multiple patterns to find the best ratio
