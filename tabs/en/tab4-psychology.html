Impression and psychology of colors

Learn the psychological effects of color.

What is the psychological effect of color?

Psychological effects of color refers to the influence that colors have on human emotions and behavior. Color is more than just visual information; it is ourpsychological state orbehavior pattern is deeply involved.

Areas affected by color

🧠
emotions

Colors change moods and emotions. Red brings excitement and blue brings calm.

💭
association

Colors evoke specific images. Green is associated with nature and white is associated with cleanliness.

🎯
action

Color influences purchasing behavior and decision making. Yellow attracts attention and blue increases trust.

🌡️
Sensible temperature

Warm colors make you feel warm, and cool colors make you feel cool.

🔴 Psychological effects of red color

Red

Colors that inspire passion, energy, and action

🎨 Psychological effects

  • Excitement/stimulation: Increases heart rate and secretes adrenaline
  • heads up: The most eye-catching color to attract attention
  • Action promotion: Increase purchasing desire and appetite
  • Energetic: Gives a sense of vitality and passion

💭 What is associated with

passion love energy courage fire blood danger caveat aggression

✅ Positive impression

Passionate, courageous, confident, energetic, loving, strong, determined

⚠️ Negative impression

dangerous, angry, aggressive, violent, warning, prohibited

🎯 Usage examples/usage scenarios

  • Sale campaign: Create a sense of urgency and specialness
  • CTA button: Promote actions such as “buy now”
  • Food industry: Restaurants and fast food to stimulate your appetite
  • Warning display: Error messages and warnings
  • Sports brand: Expressing vitality and energy
