🌍 Cultural differences in color

🌍 Cultural differences in color

The meaning of colors varies greatly from culture to culture. It is important to consider the culture of your target region when designing.

  • red: In Japan and China, it symbolizes festivals and good luck. In Europe and America, it has a strong meaning of danger and passion.
  • White: In Europe and America, it symbolizes purity and marriage. It is also used as a funeral color in Japan, China, and Korea.
  • yellow: Warning in Japan. In China, it is the imperial color and a symbol of nobility. In the West, it is associated with cowardice and betrayal.
  • green: A sacred color in the Islamic world. In Europe and America, it is a symbol of environmental protection and nature.

💡 On the global site, often using culturally neutral blues and greens as the main colors.

🖨️ RGB vs CMYK: Differences in screen and print colors

🖨️ RGB vs CMYK: Differences in screen and print colors

The way colors are represented in digital and printed media is fundamentally different.

Features RGB (screen) CMYK (print)
how to make colors Three primary colors of light (additive color mixture) Three primary colors (subtractive color mixture)
component Red, Green, Blue Cyan, Magenta, Yellow, Key(black)
color gamut Wide (bright colors) Narrow (dull color)
expression of white all max (255, 255, 255) No ink (paper white)
black expression All 0 (0, 0, 0) 100% K ink

Points to note in practice

  • Even if it is vivid on the monitor, it cannot be reproduced in print: Especially fluorescent colors and bright blues and greens
  • CMYK conversion preview before printing: Can be simulated with Adobe products etc.
  • Rich black: Not only K100% but also C40% M40% Y40% K100% for deep black
🎯 What is web safe color?

🎯 What is web safe color?

Web safe color is a 216-color palette guaranteed to display accurately in all browsers during the 1990s era of 8-bit color displays.

Features of Web Safe Color

  • Each RGB component has only 6 levels: 00, 33, 66, 99, CC, FF
  • Patterns like #000, #003, #006, #009, #00C, #00F in hexadecimal notation
  • Example: #FF0000 (red), #00FF00 (green), #0000FF (blue), #FFFF00 (yellow)

Significance in modern times

Modern displays can display more than 16.77 million colors (24 bits), soNo web-safe color constraints required It has become. However, it remains knowledge for the following reasons:

  • Reproduction of retro web design
  • Reference when creating GIF images limited to 256 colors
  • Basic knowledge of pallet design
🌡️ Color temperature: the science of warm and cool colors

🌡️ Color temperature: the science of warm and cool colors

color temperature represents the color of light in terms of absolute temperature (K: Kelvin), which determines psychological warmth or coldness.

warm color red, orange, yellow
neutral color green/purple
cool color blue/light blue

Psychological and physiological effects

  • Warm colors (red to yellow): Excitement, energy, increased appetite, sense time faster, increased perceived temperature
  • Cool colors (blue to green): Sedation, improves concentration, suppresses appetite, makes time seem slower, lowers perceived temperature

Practical example

  • restaurant: Warm color lighting increases appetite and creates a sense of comfort
  • Office: Cool lighting promotes concentration and relaxation
  • hospital: Expressing a sense of cleanliness and security with white to blue-green colors

🔮 Color illusion effect

🔮 Color illusion effect

Even if the color is the same, it can vary depending on the surrounding colors and placement.look different colors There is an optical illusion phenomenon.

Typical color illusion

  • Simultaneous comparison: Even the same gray color looks brighter on a black background and darker on a white background.
  • Color relative ratio: Green next to red looks more vivid green (complementary color effect)
  • Area effect: It looks dark and muddy in a small area, and bright and vivid in a large area.
  • Advance color and retreat color: Warm colors (red, orange) appear in the foreground, and cool colors (blue) appear in the background.

Use in design

  • Fine-tune button color by background color
  • For small icons, choose slightly brighter and more vivid colors.
  • Use warm colors to bring important elements to the forefront.
💻 Display gamma correction

💻 Display gamma correction

gamma correction is a technology that compensates for the differences between the physical characteristics of displays and human visual characteristics.

Difference in gamma value

  • Windows： Gamma 2.2 (standard brightness)
  • Mac： Previously, gamma was 1.8 (brighter), now unified to 2.2
  • Web standards: sRGB color space (gamma 2.2 equivalent)

Impact on practice

Color brightness may appear different on different devices and operating systems. To prevent this:

  • Check colors on multiple devices (iPhone, Android, PC, etc.)
  • Use sRGB color profile
  • Avoid extremely dark or bright colors
